## 🇺🇸 English

**What it does.** Emil Kowalski's design-engineering philosophy: the invisible details that make software feel great (press/hover states, popover origin, tooltip timing, transitions vs keyframes, clip-path, drag details).

**How it works.** A single long `SKILL.md`: philosophy, a required review format, the animation decision framework, springs, and component-building principles.

**Core takeaways.**
- Taste is trained; unseen details compound.
- Buttons respond instantly (press scale ~0.96).
- Never animate from `scale(0)`; popovers grow from their trigger.
- Skip tooltip delay on subsequent hovers.
- CSS transitions over keyframes for interruptible UI; springs for pointer-driven motion.

**When to use.** Reviewing or building a component's interactions and polish when no narrower skill fits.

**When NOT to use.** Full page art direction (use `frontend-design` / `build-awwwards-quality-sites`).
