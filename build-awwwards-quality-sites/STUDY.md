## 🇺🇸 English

**What it does.** Art-directs and builds distinctive, motion-rich marketing/portfolio/landing sites: original imagery, standout hero, GSAP choreography, exactly one smooth-scroll engine, optional Three.js, honest assets, a11y and performance.

**How it works.** 7 sections: art direction → honest asset system → hero → motion system → Three.js only with purpose → quality bar → validation before handoff. `agents/openai.yaml` is metadata for Codex.

**Core takeaways.**
- Write a compact direction before coding (thesis, hero asset, type, colour, motion narrative, scroll engine).
- Never copy reference assets/code/copy; keep asset provenance.
- GSAP as the motion system + **one** of Lenis / Locomotive; reduced motion renders final states.
- Static first frame must be complete without JS/WebGL.
- Reject fake testimonials, logo walls without proof, gradient blobs; never claim awards.

**When to use.** Portfolio, landing or editorial sites that must impress.

**When NOT to use.** Dashboards and app UIs.

## 🇧🇷 Português

**O que faz.** Direção de arte e construção de sites marcantes e animados (portfólio, landing page): hero forte, coreografia com GSAP, um único motor de rolagem suave, Three.js opcional, acessibilidade e desempenho.

**Principais lições.**
- Escrever a direção antes de codar.
- Nunca copiar assets da referência.
- GSAP + **um** motor de scroll (Lenis ou Locomotive).
- Primeiro quadro completo sem JavaScript.
- Nada de depoimentos falsos ou prêmios inventados.

**Quando usar.** Portfólio, landing pages, sites editoriais.

**Quando NÃO usar.** Dashboards e apps.
